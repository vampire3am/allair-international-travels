Good to know
before you go.

Clear answers for the parts of travel that deserve a second look.

Ask our travel desk
How much baggage is included in a student fare?

There is no single allowance for every student ticket. It depends on the airline, route, fare, eligibility and each flight segment. Ask our desk to show the exact checked and cabin allowances, number of bags and per-bag limits in your quotation. Do not assume an extra allowance until confirmed.

Can I book and pay online?

Online checkout is not available on this website yet. You can prepare a journey request and choose to send it on WhatsApp. Our desk will confirm availability, the fare, booking conditions and the available payment arrangements. A request is not a reservation or an issued ticket.

What should I check before paying for a ticket?

Check passenger names against passports, travel dates, airports, baggage on each segment, transit requirements, the total price and the fare’s change and refund rules. Request a written quotation and receipt. Payment confirmation and ticket issuance are separate steps.

What if weather affects a helicopter experience?

Weather and operational conditions can change the departure, route or landing arrangements. Ask for the operator’s rescheduling and refund conditions before paying, and leave flexibility in your itinerary.

Can you help with a group or a different destination?

Share your destination, approximate dates, group size and any important requirements with our desk. We’ll discuss what can be arranged and confirm the details before you decide.
