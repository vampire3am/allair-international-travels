Booking & privacy information

Before you confirm a booking

Ask the desk for the applicable provider and fare conditions in writing: final price and inclusions, payment deadline, baggage, changes, cancellation, refunds and any agency service fees. Availability may change until confirmed. The information on this site is guidance; your quotation and provider conditions determine your booking.

Your journey request and privacy

This planner prepares a message in your browser. It does not send your request to All Air automatically or take payment. Continuing opens WhatsApp, where you decide whether to send; WhatsApp’s privacy terms apply. Do not add passport numbers, identity documents or payment-card details to the planner. Contact our desk for the appropriate way to provide documents. Journey form details remain on this page until you edit, reset or leave it.
