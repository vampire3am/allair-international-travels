01Student travel · 6 min read

How to use your student baggage allowance properly

A 40–46 kg allowance is valuable only when your documents, fare category, and individual bag weights meet the airline’s rules.

Read article ＋

Student fares are not simply ordinary economy tickets with an extra-baggage label. Airlines normally require proof such as a valid student visa, Confirmation of Enrolment, university offer letter, or student identification. The required document depends on the carrier and destination, so send clear copies to the ticketing desk before your reservation is issued.

Pay attention to how the allowance is divided. “46 kg” often means two checked pieces of up to 23 kg each—not one bag weighing 46 kg. Cabin baggage has a separate limit, and excess size can still create charges even when the weight is acceptable. Keep medicine, documents, electronics, and one change of clothing in your cabin bag.

Before payment, ask for the baggage allowance to be shown on the confirmed itinerary. Recheck it for every segment, especially when two airlines operate the same journey. Allair can verify the fare category and baggage conditions before you commit.

02Flight planning · 5 min read

When should you request an international flight quote?

The cheapest-looking day is not always the best journey. A useful quote compares the total trip—not just the first number.

Read article ＋

Start comparing international flights as soon as your travel window becomes reasonably clear. Students should work backward from orientation and enrolment dates, while families should allow time for visas, transit requirements, and airport transfers. If your dates are flexible, share a range of two or three days rather than one fixed departure.

A proper comparison includes checked baggage, transit time, airport changes, meals, change penalties, refund rules, and the arrival time at your destination. A slightly higher fare can be better value when it avoids an overnight layover or includes an additional checked bag.

Airline inventory changes continuously, which is why screenshots and old quotations should never be treated as guaranteed prices. Ask the ticketing officer to revalidate the selected itinerary immediately before payment, then check that every passenger name matches the passport exactly.

03Kathmandu stays · 4 min read

Choosing the right Kathmandu hotel for your trip

Neighbourhood, airport access, meeting plans, and check-in times matter as much as the room photographs.

Read article ＋

Thamel works well for first-time visitors who want restaurants, shops, and heritage areas nearby. Lazimpat offers a calmer base with convenient access to central Kathmandu, while New Baneshwor is practical for business, government offices, and travellers continuing toward the eastern side of the valley.

Before reserving, confirm whether breakfast, taxes, airport pickup, extra beds, and early check-in are included. Families may value connecting rooms and lifts; business travellers may prioritise reliable Wi-Fi and meeting space; groups should check coach access and luggage storage.

Flight arrival times deserve special attention. International services can land late at night, so share your flight number with the hotel or transfer team. Allair checks current availability for your actual dates and can coordinate the room and airport vehicle as one request.

04Ground transport · 5 min read

Sedan, SUV, HiAce or Coaster: which vehicle fits?

Choose around passengers, luggage, road conditions, and comfort—not passenger count alone.

Read article ＋

A sedan is efficient for one to three travellers with ordinary luggage. Compact SUVs suit families and highway trips, while a Prado or Scorpio provides additional space and confidence for rougher roads. A HiAce is usually the comfortable middle ground for airport groups, family tours, and teams travelling with several bags.

For larger groups, a Toyota Coaster keeps everyone together and leaves more room for luggage than several small cars. A Hilux can be useful when equipment or difficult terrain is involved. Electric choices such as the BYD Atto 3 are quiet and comfortable for suitable city and highway routes.

Always provide the pickup point, destination, date, flight time, passenger total, and approximate luggage count. The Allair transport desk will then confirm the appropriate vehicle, availability, and route-specific rate before the journey.

FAQ

Common questions

How does the 40–46 kg student baggage allowance work?

As an authorised student ticketing agency, we issue student-category tickets with partnering airlines. When you provide a valid Student Visa, CoE, or University Offer Letter, the airline grants 2 pieces of 23 kg checked bags (46 kg total) — rather than the standard 30 kg single allowance — at the same fare.

Can I get a verified flight reservation for my visa application?

Yes. We generate genuine, airline-verifiable flight reservations with live PNR numbers. This satisfies VFS Global, Japan Embassy, US Consulates, and Australian Department of Home Affairs requirements — without purchasing a non-refundable ticket before your visa is approved. Issued within 30 minutes.

What happens if weather disrupts the Everest helicopter flight?

Safety comes first in the Himalayas. If poor visibility or severe mountain weather prevents flying, we reschedule to the next clear morning at no extra charge, or issue a full immediate refund — your choice.

What payment methods do you accept?

eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, Fonepay QR, direct bank transfer, international SWIFT wire, Visa & Mastercard, and cash at our Putalisadak head office.
