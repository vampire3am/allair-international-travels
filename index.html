Before you go.

A few answers to help you plan.
For the details of your trip, talk to our desk.

Can I book and pay online right now?

Online checkout and live ticketing are not available yet. The planner prepares your request for WhatsApp. All Air must check availability and provide a quote before you decide; submitting a request does not reserve a flight, room or tour.

What do you need for a flight quote?

Your departure and arrival cities, dates, one-way or return preference and passenger count. Mention children’s ages, student travel, baggage needs and flexible dates in the notes. Do not enter passport or card details in the planner.

Is extra student baggage guaranteed?

No universal allowance applies. Eligibility, route, airline and fare conditions vary, including across connecting flights. Ask for the confirmed allowance and document requirements for your specific ticket.

What is included in a holiday quote?

It depends on the itinerary. Ask the desk to list flights, hotel and room category, meals, transfers, activities, visa-related services, taxes and exclusions separately. The destination ideas on this page are starting points, not fixed packages.

Can I change or cancel my plans?

Rules depend on the airline, hotel or tour operator and the option you choose. Check deadlines, penalties, fare differences, refund conditions and agency service fees before paying. Ask the desk about your specific booking.

Can I visit the All Air office?

Yes. Find us at Star Mall, 3rd Floor, Putalisadak, Kathmandu, Sunday–Friday, 9:30 am–6:30 pm Nepal time. Call or message ahead if you want to discuss documents or a detailed itinerary.
