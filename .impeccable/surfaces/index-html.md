# Homepage redesign

Mode: Persuade, with a journey-planning task. User approved Kathmandu departure hall: crisp airport-wayfinding, deep teal, white, restrained orange, real destination photography and a clear journey planner. Code-led build explicitly selected.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A Kathmandu travel desk makes the next journey clear. Remove theatrical loading, unverified metrics, floating utility clutter and repeated generic icon-card sections.

OWN-WORLD: Deep teal navigation and service surfaces, white reading ground, warm orange actions, blue-green secondary panels. Barlow Condensed headings and Source Sans 3 body. Rectangular photography and crisp readable form fields; restrained corner language.

STORY: Visitors understand flights, student support and holidays, explore actual trip ideas, and prepare a detailed journey request. Live payment and ticketing are not represented as available before merchant/inventory onboarding.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim utility line and clean branded navigation above a broad Himalayan photograph with a large concise heading. A white journey planner occupies the right third beside the image and remains usable without scrolling on desktop. A grounded service strip closes the opening. Signature interaction: selecting a destination pre-fills the planner and highlights the selected route, with native smooth scrolling respecting reduced motion.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3, Kathmandu departure hall; seed 54956b34. Concourse challenger competitive on route clarity but weaker on holiday inspiration. Paper menagerie/origami/datamatics/punk/manual declined on audience identification and product clarity; retained only motion consistency, progressive disclosure, hierarchy, composition variety and grouped detail discipline. Other grounded candidates: travel publication, itinerary desk, student departure guide, route atlas, hotel concierge, travel folder. Category-default private-jet/stat-chip hero rejected by the user request.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
